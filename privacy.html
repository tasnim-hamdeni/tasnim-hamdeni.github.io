Classroom privacy

Classroom records

The classroom stores student names, email addresses, course enrollments, attendance, submitted work, project entries, and teacher feedback. Tasnim manages these records for teaching. Students see their assigned course and their own attendance and submissions. Shared project tables show the class’s project entries; teacher-only fields stay private.

Storage and access

The website uses GitHub Pages, a hosted classroom backend, a private D1 database, and the owner’s Google Drive. Google access is limited to files created or explicitly selected for this app. Files are served through permission checks; student submissions are not shared through public Drive links. Google credentials are encrypted on the backend and are not sent to students. Sign-in sessions remain in browser memory and expire after eight hours.

Existing public documents

Previously published GitHub lesson documents remain public. New classroom uploads use private storage.

Retention and controls

Automatic yearly deletion is not enabled in this test version. Contact Tasnim through your normal class channel to request corrections, access, or deletion. The owner can revoke Google access in Google Account permissions. Revocation stops future access; it does not itself delete classroom records or previously stored files. Hosting providers may retain logs and recovery copies under their own policies.

No advertising

The app does not use classroom data for advertising or AI model training. Email delivery is disabled. Infrastructure providers may process technical request information needed to operate the service.
